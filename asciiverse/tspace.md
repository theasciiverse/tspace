**NO FRICTION. NO BRAKES. NO MERCY.**

Out here nothing slows you down: every push of the engine stays with you
until you push back. Overshoot a corner and you bounce off the wall. Line
up a shot while you drift sideways past a pillar. Your energy is your
shield and your ammunition at once, so every shot you fire is armour you
don't have.

Fly one of four ships. The **Dart** is fast and fragile, the **Lance**
does a bit of everything, the **Anvil** is slow, tough and carries
bouncing bombs, and the **Weaver** sows mines and fires shots that bounce
off walls. Grab **Overcharge**, **Scatter**, **Shroud** and **Overdrive**
from the arena floor. First pilot to 15 kills takes the match.

Up to eight pilots fight in the Foundry. Bots fill the empty seats at
three skill levels, or friends join you on the same network or across
the internet with an invite ticket. One of you hosts, and no server is
needed.

Every ship, spark and star is drawn in Braille dots.

**Mind your momentum.**

## Controls

- **W** or **Up**: thrust, **S** or **Down**: reverse; **A** **D** or
  **Left** **Right**: turn
- **Space**: guns, **F**: bomb, **X**: mine, **E**: afterburner
- **1** to **4**: change ship; **Tab**: scores; **Esc**: menu; **H**: help

Best in a terminal that reports key releases (kitty, foot, WezTerm,
Ghostty and others), so you can thrust, turn and fire at once.

## Options

- `--play`: straight into a game against bots; `--demo`: watch them
- `--host`, `--join`, `--join TICKET`: play together
- `--bots N`, `--skill easy|medium|hard`, `--ship NAME`, `--mute`
